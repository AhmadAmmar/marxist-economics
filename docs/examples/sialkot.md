# Worked example: Sialkot footballs

Sialkot, in Pakistan, is a major centre of football production. This example follows one stitched ball from the worker's piece rate to the retail price, using the pamphlet's categories and published figures.

The figures come from different sources and years, each labelled. Together they illustrate the relations, and they are not the accounts of one firm. The numbers are shared with [section 5.8 of the pamphlet](../chapters/05-labour-power-wages-surplus-labour-and-surplus-value.md#subsec-sialkot-football-stitching) and the [mathematics guide](../mathematics.md).
